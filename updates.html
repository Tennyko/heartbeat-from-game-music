4 October 2026 · Reproducible development records

Audio renders now retain input files, settings, software versions and output records. A local listening-history page collects older and newer versions. Public updates remain selected milestones.

3–4 October 2026 · Streaming system integration

Continuous music transformation has been connected to recorded heart-rate replay and short live-watch runs. A five-minute replay completed without playback-buffer underruns. Synthetic descending-heart-rate input covered the final transition and stopping path.

Long-duration audio stability, end-to-end latency, musical beat alignment and listening quality still require evaluation. A separate 30-minute HR acquisition test contained temporary data gaps.

30 September 2026 · Cross-author music expansion

Twelve CC0 tracks from twelve additional authors were added with source and licence records. Seven provisionally passed technical screening; five remain pending rhythm review. Three were assigned to development; nine remain external evaluation candidates.

September 2026 · Source audit and development corpus

The earlier 19-track corpus comes from one CC0 source pack. Sixteen provisionally passed screening. These tracks remain development material; broader evaluation uses additional authors and measurable acoustic conditions.

Updated 4 October 2026. Technical checks and selected development listening do not establish perceptual or physiological effects.
